Sijie Kong

I am a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Cornell University. Previously, I completed my Master's degree at UC Santa Barbara, where I worked with the UCSB ArchLab under the guidance of Jonathan Balkind.

My interests are in programming languages, formal methods and computer architecture. I work on open-source hardware compilers, synthesis tools and accelerator design.
